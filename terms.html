Effective Date: May 27, 2026

The Short Version: We prioritize your privacy. This converter runs entirely in your web browser. Your files are never uploaded to our servers, never seen by us, and never stored by us.

1. Privacy & Data Handling

Unlike traditional online converters, this tool utilizes WebAssembly (Wasm) technology to process files locally on your device.

  • No Server Uploads: When you select a file, it remains in your browser's memory. It is not transmitted over the internet to any backend server.
  • No Data Retention: Since we do not receive your files, we cannot store, copy, or view them. Once you close the tab, all data is instantly cleared from your browser's memory.
  • Cookies: We do not use tracking cookies or store personal data. We use "Local Storage" only to remember your preference for Dark Mode.
